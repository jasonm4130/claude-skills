# Design brief (blind designers)

Read: `run/BRIEF.md` (mission, decision map, rules), `run/key-claims.md` (the verified claims, build from these), `run/outline.md`.
Open `run/board.md` or `run/kb/` only to check a key claim against its source.
Do NOT read other designers' files in `run/designs/`. Never open `run-private/`.

## Deliverable: `run/designs/<your-id>.md` (at most ~200 lines)
1. Summary: one paragraph, in plain words, of what changes for the user.
2. A decision for each D#, citing the key claims it rests on by id (`[K3]`) and saying what each claim says.
3. What gets built or changed, as an ordered list of steps each small enough to review on its own. Mark which need the user's approval.
4. What gets removed or left alone. Removing or changing nothing are valid answers.
5. Prior art: 1–3 existing systems this resembles, and what failed for them that this avoids.
6. Risks: what would prove the design wrong, and the cheapest test for each.
